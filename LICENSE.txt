2. PRIVACY - WE COLLECT AND STORE NOTHING

 - The developer does not collect, store, or receive any of your data.
 - No account, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
 - The app never connects to any server owned by the developer.
 - Screenshots are not saved to your disk. A capture is placed on your
   Windows clipboard, and a temporary web page used to hand the image to your
   browser is created in your Temp folder and deleted automatically within
   about a minute.
 - The only setting the app keeps is the optional "Start with Windows" entry
   in your own user registry. The uninstaller removes it.


3. HOW BING IS USED (PLEASE NOTE)

The purpose of this app is to search your screenshot with Microsoft Bing Visual
Search. When you capture, your web browser sends the selected image directly to
Microsoft Bing. That transfer is between you and Microsoft and is covered by
Microsoft's own privacy statement; the developer never sees it. Only capture
content you are comfortable sending to Bing.
